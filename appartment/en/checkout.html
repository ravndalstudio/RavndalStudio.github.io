Check-out Instructions

  • Gather used towels: Place used towels in the laundry basket on top of the washing machine in the bathroom.
  • Take out the trash: Leave the apartment tidy and place trash in the container under the kitchen sink.
  • Turn off appliances: Switch off the iron, coffee maker and stove before departure for fire safety.
  • Lock the door: Close and lock all windows and doors when you leave the apartment.
  • Waste key: Leave the trash container key in the kitchen drawer.
  • Dishwasher: If you load the dishwasher and start it before you leave, we appreciate it.
  • Log out: Remember to log out of all personal streaming accounts on the smart TV.
