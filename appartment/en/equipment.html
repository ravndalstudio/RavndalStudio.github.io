Apartment Equipment

Washing machine:

  • Pour detergent into the left compartment (prewash in the right compartment when needed). Detergent is stored in the bathroom cart.
  • Turn the program selector to the desired wash cycle (e.g. Cotton) and choose temperature using the temp button.
  • Press the Play button to start, located to the right of the display.
  • The door cannot be opened until the cycle is finished. To cancel, select "Spin-Pump" to drain the water first, then press "Stop" and wait for the door to unlock.
  • Turn the washing machine off using the On/Off selector.

Washing machine user manual

Dryer:

  • Check that the water drawer is empty before starting.
  • Turn the program selector to the desired cycle (e.g. Cotton).
  • Press the Play/Pause button (right button on the display) to start.
  • To open the door: press the Play/Pause button to stop the dryer.
  • Turn the dryer off by setting the program selector to On/Off.
  • Empty the water drawer after use and replace it.

Dryer user manual

Dishwasher: The quick program is recommended.

TV & streaming: Log in with your own accounts.

Heating: The apartment has heated floors in the living room and bathroom, adjusted by the thermostat.
