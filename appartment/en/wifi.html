WiFi and TV

WiFi: The apartment has wireless internet. The WiFi name and password are sent before arrival.

Smart TV: The TV is connected to the Altibox set-top box and includes all standard channels and services available from Altibox TV Content.

The Smart TV also supports streaming apps such as Netflix, HBO and YouTube, but these require login with your own streaming account.

If you log in with your own streaming accounts, please remember to log out before you leave.
