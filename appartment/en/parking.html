Directions

When driving down from Strømsbrua, keep to the left and then turn left to drive under the bridge. Follow the road further, and you will see the building on the left side. See the map below for more information.
Note: The iPhone Maps app does not show the correct address; use the Google Maps app instead.

Guest parking

Follow the main road past all buildings in Paradis Brygge, turn left, and park in the outdoor parking area behind the building.

Reserved parking

The apartment has a reserved parking space in the basement. Entrance is from the main road at the end of the building.

Garage door: Opened with the same code as for the main entrance. The keypad is located next to the door.
Parking space: When you arrive, turn left and drive nearly to the end. On the left, you will see "104" on the wall. The parking space is on the right side and is marked "104 H0101" on the ceiling. In the image, it is the space where a white Audi Q6 is parked.
Entrance from garage: On the wall marked 104, you will find the entrance door, which opens with the door code. Go up one floor and turn left.

Important: Use only the space marked "104 H0101".
