Local Area Tips

Grocery shopping
Kiwi and Coop Extra are 7–10 minutes away on foot and offer groceries such as fruit, bread, coffee, mineral water, beer, and dinner ingredients. For international food products, try Lille Asia and EkoMarket nearby.

Transport tips
For bus and train timetables, check Kolumbus.no. The easiest way to buy tickets is the Kolumbus mobile app.
Train: Paradis station – 6–7 minutes on foot. Frequent departures to Stavanger city center, Sandnes and the airport.
Train to Stavanger city center takes about 2 minutes.
Train to Sandnes city center takes about 3 minutes.
Bus: Strømsbrua stop – 7–8 minutes on foot. Buses go toward Stavanger, Hillevåg, Sandnes and Forus.
Bus to Stavanger city center takes about 5 minutes.
Bus to Sandnes city center takes about 30 minutes.
Bus to the airport takes about 50 minutes.

Cafés and food
There are several cafés and take-away options within 10–15 minutes walking distance, including pizza, burgers, sushi, Asian food and local cafés. For a larger restaurant selection, Stavanger city center is just a 2-minute train ride away and offers everything from casual lunch spots to fine dining. You can also order food delivery to the door using Foodora or Wolt.

Activities
Gym: Evo Paradis – 10–11 minutes walking distance.
Walking routes: Hillevågsvannet, Strømvik, Godalen, Storhaug and Våland. Great areas for short and longer walks.

Swimming and beach
Godalen beach – 15–20 minutes on foot, or take the bus. One of Stavanger’s most popular swimming spots.
Sauna: 2 minutes on foot – book time at Damp.

Useful services
Pharmacy: Vitusapotek Paradis – 10–12 minutes on foot.
Vinmonopolet: Kilden shopping center in Hillevåg – a short bus ride away.
