1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Ravndal Studio handles information when you use Equation Builder (the "Application").

2. Data We Process

Equation Builder is designed to work without creating an account.

  • Gameplay data on device: Progress and settings are stored locally on your device.
  • Technical diagnostics: Platform providers may process anonymized crash or performance information to keep the app stable.
  • Store and platform data: Apple App Store and Google Play may process transaction and device data under their own policies.

3. How Data Is Used

  • To run core game functionality.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve quality.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Third-Party Services

Distribution and in-app transactions are handled by Apple and Google according to their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Children's Privacy

Equation Builder is not intended to collect personal information from children knowingly.

6. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to support@ravndalstudio.com.
