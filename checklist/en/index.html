New Version 2: Cloud Sync & Collaboration

Version 2 introduces our new cloud and sharing platform. This lets you synchronize your checklists across all your devices and collaborate with others in real time. The solution is tailored for coordinating complex routines related to cabins, RVs, boats, technical maintenance and professional workflows. Automatic backups ensure your critical processes are always protected.

The Pro subscription includes full access to cloud features, real-time collaboration and unlimited checklists. Existing Pro users will receive these new capabilities at no additional cost.

We continue to offer the Standard plan as a one-time purchase for those who prefer a local solution. This plan removes ads and increases the checklist limit, but does not include cloud sync, backup or sharing. The Free version remains available with core functionality.

Launch: Version 2 is planned for release in Q1 2026.
